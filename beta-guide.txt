TEKNIK ODA BETA GUIDE

Thanks for helping. The test takes about 30 minutes.

BEFORE YOU START
- Back up your FM save. The app never writes to it, but this is a test build.
- You need: Windows 10/11 (64-bit), FM27 (Steam, Epic Games or Game Pass). 16 GB RAM recommended.

INSTALL
1. Close FM.
2. Download the installer from teknikodafm.com and run it. If Windows shows a warning, choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. When Teknik Oda opens, go to Settings > In-game plugin and press "Install plugin". Approve if Windows asks for permission.
4. Open FM. The first launch with the plugin can take a few minutes. This is normal.
5. Load your career. Teknik Oda reads the save by itself; the first read can take about half a minute.

The app follows your Windows language. To switch, use Settings > Language.

WHAT I WOULD LIKE YOU TO CHECK
1. Did the install and the plugin work? How many minutes did FM's first launch take?
2. Do the next match and league table on the Office screen match FM?
3. Pick three players: do CA, role stars, value and contract match FM?
4. Advance one game day. Did Teknik Oda pick up the new date by itself?
5. Walk through the screens. Is anything empty, broken or wrong? Is any text still in Turkish?
6. Did the game slow down or close?
7. Open a player profile in FM. Did the "Open profile" and "Compare" buttons appear and work?
8. Do you use a face or logo pack? Are the counts right under Settings > Player photos and logos?
9. Look at the Teknik Oda card at the bottom right in FM. Is its advice sensible for your own player and for a player at another club? If you disagree with it, take a screenshot.
10. If an offer comes in for one of your players, did the card open in FM? Are the fee and club right? Do the decision and the suggested figure make sense?
11. Did the briefing card appear in FM the day before a match? Are the opponent's form, absences and the rest suggestion right?
12. Open "Season card" in the League race box on the Office screen. Do the numbers match FM? Would you share it?
13. Do you use a custom skin (FMU, CTRL, ASF...)? Which one? Do the buttons and cards show with it?
14. If you play on Epic Games or Game Pass: on the Diagnostics screen, is the platform right? Was the save read and did the plugin install? This test is especially valuable to us.

IMPORTANT
Do not press "Open in game" during a match.

PLEASE SEND ME
1. Your club, league and FM language.
2. Your computer's RAM.
3. Short answers to the points above, with screenshots.
4. Problem report: press "Report a problem" at the bottom left and attach TeknikOda-Rapor.txt.
   If the app does not open at all, run "Teknik Oda Diagnostics" from the Start menu.
   Your Windows user name is hidden in the file; your club and manager name may appear. You can read it before sending.
5. The thing you liked most and the thing you liked least.
6. Would you pay monthly for this app? How much?
