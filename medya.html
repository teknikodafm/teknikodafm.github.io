Short description

Teknik Oda is an independent Windows app that reads your Football Manager career live. It opens a card inside FM when an offer arrives, briefs you on the opponent before a match, and brings squad, scouting, opposition analysis and finances into one app. It is available in English and Turkish and knows the Turkish Süper Lig foreign player rule.
